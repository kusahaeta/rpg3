ゲームの進み具合（セーブデータ）と設定は、お使いの端末の中にだけ保存します。端末の外へは送りません。アプリを削除すると、セーブデータも消えます。

本アプリには、広告、アクセス解析、クラッシュの報告などの外部サービスは入っていません。ほかのアプリやウェブサイトをまたいだトラッキングもしません。

お子さまの利用

本アプリは情報を集めないため、お子さまの情報を集めることもありません。

Nyanko Fantasy Privacy Policy

October 20, 2026

"Nyanko Fantasy ~ From the Hill Where the Star Fell ~" (the "App") does not collect any personal information from its players.

The App does not send your name, email address, location, device identifiers, play history, or any other information to the developer or to any third party. The App does not connect to the internet, and there is no account registration.

Your game progress (save data) and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent off the device. Deleting the App also deletes your save data.

Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

The App contains no external services such as advertising, analytics, or crash reporting. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any information, it does not collect any information from children.
